## 4. Evidence Consistency Engine (ECE)

> **Important scientific framing:**
> The ECE is a **prototype** trained on **300 programmatically generated synthetic
> scenarios** with hand-authored root-cause rankings.
>
> Ground-truth labels were NOT sourced from real epidemiological datasets.
> The training data was designed to reflect plausible causal relationships, but
> these are the authors' assumptions — not empirically validated ground truth.
>
> Claims made for the ECE:
> - **Can make**: "Detects conflicts between visual symptoms and environmental conditions"
> - **Can make**: "Provides a ranked list of plausible causal hypotheses"
> - **Cannot make**: "Validated causal reasoning from real field data"
> - **Cannot make**: "Statistically proven improvement over simple baselines on real data"

> synthetic context. This requires investigation with real field data.

## 5. Limitations

1. **PlantVillage domain gap**: All 5,667 training images are lab-controlled. Performance on real farm photography is unknown and likely lower.
2. **ECE synthetic training**: The Evidence Consistency Engine is evaluated on scenarios generated by the same rule-based system used to design its training data. This is circular — real epidemiological validation is required.
3. **Small per-class test sets**: Classes like `apple_scab_leaf` (8 samples), `cherry_leaf` (5 samples), and `corn_gray_leaf_spot` (6 samples) produce statistically unreliable metrics.
4. **No OOD detection**: The model will confidently classify non-leaf images (e.g., a rock, a shoe). A confidence threshold of 0.35 is used as a basic guard.
5. **Calibration**: ECE = 0.312 indicates the model is overconfident. Temperature scaling has not yet been applied.

- "Our MobileNetV3-Small achieves 77.6% accuracy on a held-out PlantVillage benchmark"

- "Our Evidence Consistency Engine detects cases where visual symptoms and environmental conditions are inconsistent"

- ~~"94.2% accuracy"~~ (fabricated)
- ~~"Validated causal reasoning"~~ (not empirically validated)
- ~~"Pixel-level robustness testing"~~ (feature-space perturbations only)
- ~~"Statistically significant improvement"~~ (test set too small for reliable significance)
